# Intro
Purrr Voice Channel is a tube channel strip in the style of the ART Voice Channel: tube preamp, expander or gate, compressor or limiter, de-esser and a 4 band EQ in one window. It is free and open source (AGPL-3.0-or-later), made by Studio 87.

# Status
Version 0.1 is the first plugin build (VST3 and a standalone app for Windows, macOS and Linux). Its window is the approved design mockup (docs/mockup/index.html): the faceplate picture is taken from the mockup and every knob, switch, display and meter is drawn live on top at the same place. Its audio engine is the mockup's engine ported line by line to C++; a test plays the same signals through both and they match within -144 dB (rounding only). The pictures in this manual were taken from the mockup. The preview rig at the bottom of the mockup (test signal, Play, Load file, Before / After, Match level, Hear the difference) is not part of the plugin. Not yet checked in a real DAW: please report anything odd. Only the default colour look is in the plugin so far.

# Install
- Windows: run the Setup .exe from the windows folder. The VST3 goes to Common Files\VST3, the app and this manual to Program Files\Studio 87\Purrr Voice Channel. Remove it from Settings, Apps; it asks before deleting your presets.
- macOS: open the .pkg from the macos folder (not notarized: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway). Remove it with "Uninstall Purrr Voice Channel" in Applications.
- Linux, Flatpak DAW: flatpak install --user the .flatpak file from the linux folder, then rescan. Native DAW: put the Purrr Voice Channel.vst3 folder from the tarball into ~/.vst3.
- Presets live in your user folder under Studio 87/Purrr Voice Channel/presets (the About window shows the exact path). Everything works offline; the plugin makes no network calls.

# Under the hood
- Preamp: gain 0 to +60 dB, pad, invert, low cut, impedance tone, tube voltage (normal or high).
- Tubes: ten cat tubes on three real tube types (12AU7, 12AT7, 12AX7, new and aged), a Koren triode model with 4x oversampling and ADAA. The Gain knob drives the tube. Voicing, Colour and Variation shape how different the tubes sound.
- Dynamics: expander or gate, compressor or limiter (release 0.1 ms to 3 s), de-esser, soft knee, sidechain high-pass, auto makeup, true-peak limiter.
- EQ: 4 bands, plus or minus 15 dB, pre or post dynamics, per-band Q.
- Checking: gain reduction history, VU and peak meters with a dB scale. Before and after with level match is in the mockup's preview rig only.
- Latency: 27 samples for the tube oversampling plus about 1.5 ms of limiter lookahead (72 samples at 48 kHz), reported to the DAW so tracks stay in time.
- Fixed in 0.1: the High tube voltage now really uses the high-voltage tube curve (before it only changed gain and bandwidth); three filter constants follow the sample rate.
- All voicing is our own; nothing is measured from any real tube or unit.
