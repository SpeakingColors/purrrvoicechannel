Purrr Voice Channel

A tube channel strip in the style of the ART Voice Channel: tube preamp, expander/gate, compressor/limiter, de-esser and 4 band EQ. Free and open source, by Studio 87.

Status: design mockup, no plugin and no release yet. The page below is the approved design running in your browser; the plugin is not built. The manual (PDF) is an interface tour of this mockup.

Below is the playable design mockup: press Play, switch tubes, turn Gain, Voicing and Colour. Open it full screen · Source on GitHub · Support Studio 87 on Ko-fi
